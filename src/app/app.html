Your knowledge, in plain Markdown you own.

A native, local-first notes app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Built with SwiftUI, it opens your Obsidian vaults as-is.

A vault is just a folder.

No database, no lock-in. Every note is a Markdown file any other app can read — including Obsidian, whose wikilinks, .canvas and .base files Netherite understands. Keep your vault in iCloud Drive to sync between devices, or anywhere on disk. 

~/iCloud Drive/Notes
├── Daily/
│   └── 2026-10-05.md
├── Projects/
│   └── Netherite launch.md
├── Ideas.canvas
├── Books.base
└── .netherite/
    └── themes/
